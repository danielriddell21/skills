---
name: lgtm
description: The user says "lgtm", "looks good to me" or "ship it" about something you just showed them (a plan, diff, option list). Treat it as approval of exactly what was shown and carry on.
---
"LGTM" approves what was on screen, no more.

- Proceed with the plan, diff or option you last presented. Do not ask again about the same thing.
- It covers only what you showed. A new action, a wider scope, or anything riskier than described needs its own OK (see `ask-dont-guess`).
- Say in one line what you are now doing, then do it.
- If several things were pending and "LGTM" is ambiguous about which, ask which, once.

The `/lgtm` command (this plugin) is different: it pre-approves things for one turn. See its README.
